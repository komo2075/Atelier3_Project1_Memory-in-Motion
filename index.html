Memory in Motion

An Interactive 3D Spatial Memory Archive

About the Project

Memory in Motion is an interactive 3D memory archive that explores the relationship between technology, physical space, and personal memory.

The project transforms a familiar campus environment into a digital space for remembering. Instead of organizing photographs in a traditional album, I use a 3D campus model as a spatial interface. Memory points are placed at different locations in the environment. Each point connects a physical place with photographs and experiences from my time with friends and classmates.

The project uses different forms of interaction to explore different ways of accessing memories. Users can navigate the 3D environment by moving their phones with the gyroscope. They can also interact with selected memories by rotating their phones, blowing into the microphone, or shaking the device.

These actions turn everyday phone functions into tools for discovering and revealing memories. Through this design, I explore the possibility of using mobile technology to create a more physical and spatial experience of remembering.

How to Interact

01. Explore the Map Drag the screen or enable motion control to explore the 3D campus.

02. Discover Memories Select the floating memory points to open photographs connected to different locations.

03. Rotate to Reveal Turn your phone sideways to unlock a hidden memory.

04. Blow to Reveal Blow gently toward the microphone to clear the mist covering a photograph.

05. Shake to Remember Shake your phone to reveal another moment.

06. Show or Hide Points Use the eye icon to control the visibility of memory points.
